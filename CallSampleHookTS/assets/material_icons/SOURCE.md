# Material Design Icons

The icons in this directory come from Google's Material Design Icons repository:

https://github.com/google/material-design-icons

They are distributed under the Apache License 2.0. The PNG assets are the
48-pixel (`24dp/2x`) baseline variants for `call`, `videocam`, `vpn_key`,
`person`, `login`, `logout`, `add_ic_call`, `cloud_off`,
`check_circle_outline`, and `error_outline`.
